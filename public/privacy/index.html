PriceTrack PH

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 25, 2026

PriceTrack PH is an independent price-history service for public marketplace product information. This policy explains what the PriceTrack PH browser extension and website collect, why the information is used, and how it is handled.

Information we collect

When the extension runs on a supported Shopee Philippines product page, it may collect and transmit only the public product information needed to provide price history:

  • Product URL and Shopee shop/product identifiers
  • Product title, shop name and public product image
  • Public prices, original prices, variations, SKUs and stock availability
  • The time the public product information was observed
  • A randomly generated extension installation identifier used for abuse prevention and recording limits

The installation identifier is not a Shopee account identifier. It is hashed before it is used for recording quotas.

Information we do not collect

PriceTrack PH does not collect Shopee passwords, authentication credentials, private messages, payment-card information, checkout information, or the contents of unrelated webpages. A Shopee account is not required to use the extension.

How information is used

  • Build and display public product price history
  • Track price and stock changes for product variations
  • Prevent duplicate records, spam and excessive automated submissions
  • Maintain, secure and improve the PriceTrack PH service

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell user data. Information is processed only to operate PriceTrack PH. Hosting and database providers, including Vercel and Supabase, process information on our behalf as necessary to provide the service. Public marketplace product details and price history may be displayed publicly on PriceTrack PH.

Retention and security

Public product and price observations may be retained to provide historical comparisons. Abuse-prevention records are retained only as needed to enforce recording limits and protect the service. Information is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to administrative database credentials is restricted to the server-side collector and those credentials are not included in the extension.

Your choices

You can stop collection by disabling or uninstalling the extension. You may also disable completion notifications in the extension popup. For questions or removal requests concerning submitted information, contact PriceTrack PH through the contact form on the website.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

PriceTrack PH uses information only to provide or improve its disclosed single purpose. We do not use or transfer user data for personalized advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to product price history. Our use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact messages and attachments

If you contact PriceTrack PH, the name, email address, subject, message, and optional attachment you provide are sent through our email provider. Attachments are not placed in public product-history records or stored in Supabase Storage. A copy may remain in the PriceTrack PH contact mailbox and the email provider according to their normal retention practices.

Visit pricetrackph.com and use the Contact option in the footer.
